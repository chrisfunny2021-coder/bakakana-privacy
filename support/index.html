常见问题

需要注册账号吗？
不需要。学习记录只保存在你的设备上。

哪些内容免费？
あ行、か行可以免费使用全部功能，学完后自动解锁さ行、た行。其余内容需要一次性购买“全部解锁”，不是订阅。

换了手机，购买还在吗？
在。用同一个 Apple ID 登录后，在「我的」→「设置」里点“恢复购买”即可。学习进度不会跨设备同步。

怎么清除学习记录？
「我的」→「设置」→「重置学习记录」。

猫说话太凶了？
可以在设置里调低毒舌程度。本猫的话都是玩笑，认真的只有五十音。

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Your progress is stored only on your device.

What is free?
The あ and か rows are free with every feature, and the さ and た rows unlock once you finish them. Everything else is a one-time "Unlock All" purchase. There is no subscription.

I got a new phone. Is my purchase still there?
Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore Purchase in Me → Settings. Study progress does not sync between devices.

How do I reset my progress?
Me → Settings → Reset Progress.

The cat is too rude.
You can turn the rudeness down in Settings. The cat's insults are a joke. The kana are serious.
